## 0.1.0-alpha.1 — The Perimeter (November 2026)

The first playable alpha.

**The Zone:** one seamless open world about 13 by 28 km, no loading screens, from the army's fence in the south to
the plant in the north: Old Hamlet and the Southern Perimeter, Reedwater Marsh, Blackwater Gorge, the Agrarian
Institute, the Scrapyard, Hollow Valley, Lake Kalina, Molot Works (the Furnace bar), the Rassokha vehicle
graveyard, Sulfur Town, Depot 17, Lesnoe Farmstead, Zarechny, the Red Forest, the Duga Array, the Abandoned
Sanatorium, the Zaton backwaters, Jupiter and Yanov Station, Pripyat and the Chernobyl NPP with its cooling towers
and sarcophagus. Roads, the railway, rivers and lakes, pine and birch forest, thousands of buildings to search.

**A-Life:** hundreds of stalker squads and mutant packs live on the whole map whether you are near them or not:
they travel between camps, bases, ruins, lairs and anomaly fields, fight each other, die in emissions and are
replaced from their factions' bases. The PDA's news tells you what happened.

**Guns:** the Battle of Spades arsenal on Zone rules: hit-scan, recoil, damage falloff, aiming down the sights.
Fit and strip optics, suppressors, grips, lights, lasers, grenade launchers and magazines at the weapon bench (U).
Change rounds (FMJ, AP, slug, flechette). Worn guns jam. Underbarrel launchers (V).

**Survival, Misery-style:** weight slows you, stamina runs out, radiation builds up, wounds bleed, you need food,
water and sleep, and the psy field near the Array eats your mind. Suits and helmets protect against bullets,
fire, shocks, acid, radiation and psy; artifacts on the belt help (and irradiate).

**Anomalies, artifacts, emissions:** burners, electros, vortices, acid and psy fields; throw bolts to find them.
Detectors (Echo, Bear, Veles) find the artifacts they hide. Every 45 minutes or so an emission turns the sky red:
get under a roof or underground.

**Your bunker:** the hatch in Old Hamlet leads down to your own concrete room in solid earth. Dig it out, wall
it, light it and furnish it with what you bring back.

**The story:** you wake in Old Hamlet with one task on your PDA: find the Ferryman.

**Co-op:** split screen for up to four, LAN and online co-op, and the dedicated server, with full controller
support everywhere.

- Removed Herobrine.
