# Changelog

Release notes for Exclusion, newest first. The release workflow (`.github/workflows/release.yml`) publishes the
section whose heading starts with the tag's version as the GitHub release notes. Keep it in step with
`assets/news/news.dqn` (the launcher's news). Patch notes never mention Herobrine and every entry ends with
"Removed Herobrine." (The engine's own history is in DOKUQUEST_CHANGELOG.md.)

## 0.1.0-alpha.8 — Patch (November 2026)

- **No more block-game pop-ups in the Zone:** skill levels, perks, Exploring, place discoveries and achievements no
  longer show up; only the Zone's own messages remain.
- **No death compass:** dying in the Zone never hands you the base game's compass.
- **Rad rats are slower:** a sprinting stalker can now outrun a swarm (about 70% of your sprint); they still swarm.
- **Ladders:** your gun is slung while you climb and only your own two hands reach up the rungs, hand over hand;
  the gun comes back up when you step off.
- **Head torch:** wear it in the inventory's new LAMP slot, then switch it with the torch key (L / d-pad up); it
  throws a beam ahead of you. Without one worn, the key tells you so.
- **Main menu music:** the menu plays the Zone's own quiet mood (the drone or the campfire guitar), never the block
  game's theme.
- **Gun sounds:** a gun without a sound class no longer plays an empty sound.
- Removed Herobrine.

## 0.1.0-alpha.7 — Patch (November 2026)

- **No more houses on stilts:** where your world's ground dips under a building, the gap under its walls and floor is
  filled with a stone or concrete foundation (short gaps only: eaves, porches and bridges stay open).
- **The story ends at the Generators:** the last step of FIND THE FERRYMAN now leads past the plant, over the river,
  to the Generators instead of the sarcophagus; the ending is set there.
- **Emissions near the plant:** at CNPP North / South and the Generators the wave hits earlier and harder.
- **The Generators' energy spheres hum:** an electric buzz you hear as you come near one.
- Removed Herobrine.

## 0.1.0-alpha.6 — Your Zone (November 2026)

**The Zone is your world:** the map is rebuilt from your own hand-made Zone, at about 12 by 9 km, with the places
1 to 1.5 km apart like S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Anomaly. Every hill, river, road, tree and building shape comes from your
world and is rebuilt with Exclusion's blocks, then worn down: moss, rust, broken windows, crumbled wall tops, rubble.
- **STALKER places and names:** Great Swamps, Cordon, Darkscape, Meadow, Garbage, Agroprom, Dark Valley, Rostok,
  Wild Territory, Yantar, Army Warehouses, Dead City, Truck Cemetery, Red Forest, Limansk, Radar, Jupiter and Yanov
  Station, Zaton, Pripyat, the Deserted Hospital, CNPP and the Generators, joined by roads and tracks. Agroprom
  Underground, X-16 and X-18 have hatches down to small labs.
- **Life:** rookie camps round campfires, bandit posts, a military checkpoint, mutant lairs and squads on the roads.
  Anomaly fields everywhere, three of them by the Cordon bridge near the start. Trees and plants sit on the ground.
- **Overgrowth:** denser forests, poplar rows, bushes, tall grass, flowers, ferns, dead weeds and reeds.
- **Looting like the games:** no chests. Search wardrobes, fridges, lockers, desks, cupboards, toolboxes, weapon
  cases and first-aid boxes; smash wooden crates; pick up items lying on tables, shelves and floors. Most spots
  hold little or nothing. Rooms are dressed with bottles, mess tins, kettles, candles and papers.
- **Animations:** first person sways, bobs, sprints with the gun across the chest, eases into the sights, kicks
  with recoil, reloads mag by mag or shell by shell, racks jams, throws bolts, and eats, drinks, bandages and
  injects like Misery. Stalkers carry their guns at low ready, aim, crouch, flinch, fall and stay down as bodies,
  and sit round campfires. Mutants gallop, waddle, stalk and lunge.
- **Real scale:** items in the world and in hands are their real size.
- **Beds and sleeping bags** are two blocks long. Roofs sit on their walls, and the knife is held properly.
- Removed Herobrine.

## 0.1.0-alpha.5 — The Real Zone (November 2026)

**The Zone, from the map:** the world is laid out on a satellite map of the real Zone, about 13 by 34 km: the plant
and its cooling pond in the north, Pripyat beside them, the Red Forest, villages where the real ones stand, the
rivers, the lake in the south-east, the railway and a road network that joins every place. Buildings have finer
detail: pitched roofs, ragged broken walls, cornices, sills, steps and rubble.

**Life in the Zone:** stalkers wear their faction's colours and stay put; mutant packs and stalker bands roam near
you. Talk to any friendly stalker for rumours, trade or work. Traders and camp leaders give jobs: hunt a pack,
clear out a gang, bring an artifact or trophies, find a stash. Dead stalkers leave a pack to search, sometimes with
a PDA that marks a stash. Every faction remembers what you do to its people.

**Anomalies:** twenty kinds, each with its own look, sound and trap: pulls, shoves, flame jets, wandering fire,
arcs, ball lightning, acid pools, gas, blisters, glass shards, burning moss, poppies, will-o'-wisps and more. Throw a
bolt first. Five new artifacts.

**The PDA:** a rugged handheld in both hands, with Map, Tasks, Statistics, Relations, Contacts, Guide, Radio and
Messages; the map reads like aerial imagery, with buildings, task areas, stashes and places.

**Emissions and atmosphere:** a red sky racing in from the plant, forked lightning, a burning wave front across the
sky, the wave's roar and boom and your ears ringing after; clear daylight with distant haze, moonlit nights your head
torch cuts through, storms. Every gun class sounds its own, with echoes outside and a dry crack indoors.

**Survival:** cooking at campfires and stoves, a workbench for repairs, bandages, bolts and kits; no block-game
furnaces or crafting screens. You start with a head torch. How to Play is about the Zone, and death looks like it.

- The engine follows DokuQuest's newest changes, with its network and LAN fixes.
- Fixed a crash when an emission caught creatures outdoors.
- Removed Herobrine.

## 0.1.0-alpha.2 — Into the Zone (November 2026)

**First person, the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. way:** gloved hands and sleeves in your suit's colours hold every gun by its grip
and handguard, sway with your view, lower when you sprint and rise when drawn. The knife slashes. The PDA is a real
device in both hands, its map a zoomable relief of the Zone. Detectors are held up in your left hand with a bolt
ready in the right, and only work in hand: Echo's lamps, Bear's needle, Veles' radar sweep.

**You look the part:** a hooded stalker by default, every suit its own look, helmets and gas masks on your head.
Stalkers wear their faction's colours in eight looks each and carry real guns. The factions use their familiar
names: Loners, Bandits, Military, Duty, Freedom, Mercenaries, Monolith, Ecologists. New suits: the sealed anomaly
suit and army assault armour.

**Mutants:** the tusker, the hogspawn, blind scab dogs and lurchers that hunt in packs, rad rats, the near-invisible
shroud, gas-masked crawlers, husks, the puppeteer, the stoneback, the hurler and the two-headed ridgecat, each with
its own model and way of killing you. Crows. Nothing from the block game wanders the Zone any more.

**Sound:** no music out in the Zone, only its noises: wind, crows, crickets, far gunfire, howls, roars, groaning
metal, thunder, whispers in the psy fields. Emissions wail with sirens, then roar. Radios play in the bars, guitars
by the fires, and your PDA has a radio.

**Looks:** sun and moon shadows, ground fog, light shafts, darker nights for your head torch's cone, wet ground and
puddles in the rain, bloom, an overcast that comes and goes, dawn fog, the Zone's colour grade; an Effects quality
setting from Low to Ultra. New blocks and props: cracked asphalt, panel walls, peeling plaster, chain-link and
barbed wire, power lines, broken windows, radiators, rubble; thin STALKER-style trees and the rust-red Red Forest.

**Menus:** S.T.A.L.K.E.R. / Misery-style title, pause and tabbed options screens, controller-first. Searching a
crate opens a Misery-style loot screen. Quick slots and a proper crosshair replace the hotbar, hit markers show
where it came from, and you can only build in your bunker.

**Misery start:** a worn pistol, fourteen rounds and 500 roubles; Moroz gives you your first detector.

- The engine follows DokuQuest's: a daily job brings its engine changes over as a pull request.
- Removed Herobrine.

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 — The Perimeter (November 2026)

The first playable alpha.

**The Zone:** one seamless open world about 13 by 28 km, no loading screens, from the army's fence in the south to
the plant in the north: Old Hamlet and the Southern Perimeter, Reedwater Marsh, Blackwater Gorge, the Agrarian
Institute, the Scrapyard, Hollow Valley, Lake Kalina, Molot Works (the Furnace bar), the Rassokha vehicle
graveyard, Sulfur Town, Depot 17, Lesnoe Farmstead, Zarechny, the Red Forest, the Duga Array, the Abandoned
Sanatorium, the Zaton backwaters, Jupiter and Yanov Station, Pripyat and the Chernobyl NPP with its cooling towers
and sarcophagus. Roads, the railway, rivers and lakes, pine and birch forest, thousands of buildings to search.

**A-Life:** hundreds of stalker squads and mutant packs live on the whole map whether you are near them or not:
they travel between camps, bases, ruins, lairs and anomaly fields, fight each other, die in emissions and are
replaced from their factions' bases. The PDA's news tells you what happened.

**Guns:** the Battle of Spades arsenal on Zone rules: hit-scan, recoil, damage falloff, aiming down the sights.
Fit and strip optics, suppressors, grips, lights, lasers, grenade launchers and magazines at the weapon bench (U).
Change rounds (FMJ, AP, slug, flechette). Worn guns jam. Underbarrel launchers (V).

**Survival, Misery-style:** weight slows you, stamina runs out, radiation builds up, wounds bleed, you need food,
water and sleep, and the psy field near the Array eats your mind. Suits and helmets protect against bullets,
fire, shocks, acid, radiation and psy; artifacts on the belt help (and irradiate).

**Anomalies, artifacts, emissions:** burners, electros, vortices, acid and psy fields; throw bolts to find them.
Detectors (Echo, Bear, Veles) find the artifacts they hide. Every 45 minutes or so an emission turns the sky red:
get under a roof or underground.

**Your bunker:** the hatch in Old Hamlet leads down to your own concrete room in solid earth. Dig it out, wall
it, light it and furnish it with what you bring back.

**The story:** you wake in Old Hamlet with one task on your PDA: find the Ferryman.

**Co-op:** split screen for up to four, LAN and online co-op, and the dedicated server, with full controller
support everywhere.

- Removed Herobrine.
